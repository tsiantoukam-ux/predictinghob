Prediction of Human Oral Bioavailability Using Machine and Deep Learning and Model Interpretation with SHAP Values

Marina Tsiantouka · MSc Artificial Intelligence, University of Essex
HOB threshold

Human oral bioavailability (HOB) is the fraction of an oral dose that reaches the bloodstream unchanged. This dashboard shows the main results of the thesis: molecules are classified as High or Low HOB from Mordred molecular descriptors, models are chosen by 5-fold cross-validation on 1,142 training molecules, and the final models are tested once on two external test sets. The switch on the right changes the HOB threshold (50% or 20%) that separates High from Low.

Hover a chart for exact values; “Show data” opens the table behind each chart. Data: HobPre dataset (Wei et al., 2022). All values come from the thesis result files.
